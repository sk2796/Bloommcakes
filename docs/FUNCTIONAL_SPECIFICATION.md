> **Target Audience**: Business Owners, Product Managers, Bakery Operations, Customer Support, and Non-Technical Stakeholders

## 1. What is BloomCakes?

**BloomCakes** is a modern, artisanal bakery platform designed to delight customers with handcrafted cakes while giving bakery managers complete control over daily orders, deliveries, and catalog management.

The platform consists of two unified portals:
1. **The Customer Storefront**: A celebration-first shopping experience where customers can browse gourmet cakes, check delivery availability in their area, design custom cakes with photo inspirations, and checkout securely.
2. **The Bakery Admin Portal**: A command center for bakery owners, managers, and delivery personnel to monitor live revenue, update menus, manage order pipelines, and coordinate on-demand delivery couriers.

## 2. Customer Storefront Experience

The customer storefront is designed for elegance, warmth, and effortless ordering on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

### 2.1 Hero & Featured Collections
- **Visual Showcase**: Displays mouth-watering photography of signature cakes, seasonal specials, and bestsellers.
- **Bestseller Badges & Ratings**: Real-time customer satisfaction stars (e.g., 4.9 ★) and "Bestseller" tags help shoppers pick crowd favorites quickly.
- **Brand Story & Quality Guarantees**: Highlights key commitments (100% fresh baked, premium Belgian chocolate, eggless options, pure butter).

### 2.2 Catalog Browsing & Smart Filters
- **Flavor & Category Navigation**: Filter catalog by Chocolate, Fruit & Berries, Cheesecakes, Brownies, Pastries, and Vegan/Eggless treats.
- **Occasion-Based Shopping**: Browse cakes tailored for Birthdays, Anniversaries, Weddings, Baby Showers, and Festivals.
- **Instant Search**: Type any ingredient or cake name (e.g., "Truffle", "Walnut", "Lotus Biscoff") with instant live filtering.

### 2.3 Product Detail Page & Ordering Options
- **Dynamic Weight Selector**: Customers can choose cake weights (0.5 kg, 1.0 kg, 2.0 kg+). The price updates automatically on the screen.
- **Flavor & Allergen Guidance**: Clear breakdown of ingredients, allergen notices (nuts, dairy, gluten), and storage tips (e.g., "Serve chilled, store at 4°C").
- **Party Add-Ons**: Quick one-click add-ons for celebration candles, customized gift cards, and fancy sparklers.

### 2.4 Pincode Delivery Availability Checker
- Customers can enter their 6-digit pincode at any time.
- **Instant Verification**: Informs the customer immediately whether their address is within the bakery's fresh-delivery zone before they spend time customizing their order.

## 3. Interactive Custom Cake Builder

The standout feature of BloomCakes is the step-by-step **Custom Cake Builder**, allowing customers to design a one-of-a-kind cake from scratch:

```
  Step 1: Base Sponge   ──►   Step 2: Cream & Filling   ──►   Step 3: Design Style
  (Vanilla, Choc, ...)         (Ganache, Caramel, ...)        (Minimalist, Vintage)
                                                                       │
  Order in Cart         ◄──   Step 5: Photo Inspiration ◄──   Step 4: Custom Message
  (Live Price Updated)         (Upload Reference Image)       ("Happy Birthday!")
```

1. **Step 1 - Sponge Base**: Select from Classic Vanilla Bean, Dutch Chocolate, Red Velvet, or Funfetti sponge.
2. **Step 2 - Gourmet Filling**: Choose rich layer fillings such as Belgian Dark Chocolate Ganache, Salted Caramel Crunch, Tangy Raspberry Puree, or Whipped Vanilla Buttercream.
3. **Step 3 - Visual Theme & Aesthetics**: Pick design aesthetics including Vintage Lambeth piping, Pastel watercolor drip, Rustic floral botanicals, or Modern minimalist.
4. **Step 4 - Custom Message / Inscription**: Add an inscription to be piped on the cake board or chocolate plaque (e.g., *"Happy 30th Birthday Siddharth!"*).
5. **Step 5 - Reference Photo Upload**: Customers can upload a photo or sketch of an inspiration cake from Pinterest or Instagram.
   - The platform verifies the image format and securely attaches it to the order for the bakery chef to reference.
6. **Live Pricing**: As customization options are selected, the estimated total updates transparently on-screen.

## 4. Shopping Cart, Delivery Scheduling & Checkout

### 4.1 Shopping Cart Slide-Over
- View all chosen cakes, selected weights, and custom cake options.
- Adjust quantities with instant subtotal and tax calculation.
- Apply discount promotional codes (e.g., `SWEET10`, `CELEBRATE`).

### 4.2 Celebration Date & Time Slot Scheduling
- **Date Picker**: Choose delivery date (today for quick items, or pre-book days in advance).
- **Time Slot Selection**: Select preferred celebration arrival windows:
  - *Morning*: 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  - *Afternoon*: 12:00 PM – 04:00 PM
  - *Evening Party*: 04:00 PM – 08:00 PM
  - *Midnight Delivery*: (Where enabled for surprise celebrations).

### 4.3 Recipient & Gifting Options
- Customers can mark an order as a **Gift Delivery**.
- Enter separate recipient contact information and delivery instructions (e.g., *"Ring bell twice, do not spoil the surprise"*).

### 4.4 Frictionless Online Payments
- Integrated with **Razorpay** to accept all major Indian payment methods:
  - **UPI Apps**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM.
  - **Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, American Express.
  - **NetBanking & Wallets**: Direct bank transfers and digital wallets.
- Instant SMS/Email-compatible order confirmation receipt upon payment.

## 6. Bakery Operations & Admin Portal

The administrative dashboard is the nerve center of the bakery, empowering staff to run day-to-day operations smoothly.

### 6.1 Performance Overview Dashboard
- **Key Business Metrics**:
  - Live Total Revenue.
  - Total Orders Placed.
  - Total Customer Base.
  - Active Menu Items.
- **Visual Order Funnel**: Quick glance at how many orders are in each status:
  - 🟡 *Confirmed / Pending Bake*
  - 🔵 *In Transit / Shipped*
  - 🟢 *Successfully Delivered*
  - 🔴 *Cancelled*
- **Recent Orders Feed**: Highlights the latest 5 incoming orders with customer name, phone number, and total value.

### 6.2 Order Desk & Fulfillment Pipeline
- **Comprehensive Orders Manifest**: A complete, sortable table of all customer orders.
- **Search & Filter**: Search orders by Customer Name, Order ID (e.g., `BC-ORD-6096`), phone number, or date.
- **Status Lifecycle Control**: Update orders through the bakery workflow:
  1. `order_confirmed`: Kitchen receives notification and begins baking.
  2. `dispatched`: Order handed over to courier or internal driver.
  3. `delivered`: Celebration cake safely handed to customer.
  4. `cancelled`: Handled with full audit trail.
- **Kitchen Slips**: Displays exact order details, including cake weight, flavor, customer notes, and custom text inscriptions.

### 6.3 Menu & Catalog Management
- **Add New Products**: Easily introduce new seasonal cakes, brownies, or pastries with photo URL, category, price, description, and bestseller tag.
- **Automatic ID & Link Generation**: The system automatically generates friendly web links (slugs) and unique inventory codes (e.g., `BC-CAKE-BELGIAN-CHOC`).
- **Live Price & Detail Updates**: Update prices, change descriptions, or edit pictures in seconds.
- **Catalog Cleanup**: Archive or delete discontinued cakes with confirmation guards.

### 6.4 Delivery Area (Pincode) Management
- Add new delivery pincodes as the bakery expands its kitchen footprint.
- Remove or temporarily disable pincodes during heavy monsoon or out-of-coverage situations.

### 6.5 Team & Staff Roles (RBAC)
To protect sensitive financial records and ensure data privacy, the admin portal enforces four team roles:

| Staff Role | What They Can Access | Ideal For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Super Admin** | Full access to total revenue, staff management, system settings, menu, and orders. | Bakery Founders, Owners |
| **Store Manager** | Access to all orders, menu editing, delivery dispatch, and customer lists. Cannot see staff passwords or add new managers. | Kitchen Managers, Operations Heads |
| **Delivery Staff** | Access restricted to the `/admin/deliveries` screen and delivery status updates. | Delivery Coordinators, Drivers |
| **Catalog Editor** | Access restricted to updating cake photos, descriptions, and menu items. | Marketing Interns, Food Stylists |

## 7. Delivery Logistics & Multi-Carrier Coordination

BloomCakes includes an intelligent delivery coordination engine that connects the kitchen with professional couriers:

### 7.1 Delivery Provider Options
1. **Borzo (WeFast)**: Automated dispatch for rapid bike courier deliveries across the city.
2. **Porter**: Enterprise vehicle booking for fragile multi-tier wedding cakes or heavy bulk orders.
3. **Manual / In-House Delivery**: For deliveries handled by the bakery's own trusted delivery personnel.

### 7.2 Live Courier Quotes
- Bakery staff can generate a live courier fare estimate with one click before booking, helping choose the most cost-effective provider.

### 7.3 Real-Time Courier Tracking
- When an order is dispatched through Borzo or Porter, a live tracking link is attached to the delivery slip.
- Rider details (name and contact phone number) appear in the delivery management console.

## 8. Customer Safety & Data Protection

Even though the platform is user-friendly, it includes built-in protection behind the scenes:
- **Zero Account Lockouts from Mistyped Passwords**: Instead of locking legitimate customers out of their accounts after a few typos, the system uses smart pauses (exponential backoff) that stop hackers while letting real users log in smoothly.
- **Safe Customer Data**: Customer addresses and phone numbers are kept strictly inside the bakery's secure private database and are never shared or indexed.
- **Safe File Uploads**: Inspiration photos uploaded by customers are inspected to guarantee they are genuine images (PNG, JPEG, WEBP) and cannot contain malicious code.
- **Error Privacy**: If an internet glitch or temporary error happens, customers see a polite, friendly message with a reference ID rather than confusing code or technical details.
